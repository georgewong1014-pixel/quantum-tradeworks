Privacy

What this build stores, where it stores it and what leaves your device — then the notice the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 asks for.

Draft — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. It describes what this build does, read from its code, but it is not a settled policy: no operating entity has been registered to give it, and every detail shown as “To be supplied” is a gap, not a fact.

In short

The site sends nothing you enter to anyone: there are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no form that sends anything. Your browser asks this site for its own files and nothing else, and what you save stays in this browser, on your device. The one thing that travels is a page’s own address when the page is loaded — the property calculator keeps the deal’s figures in it — as set out below.

What is stored

Everything this product remembers is held in this browser’s local storage, under this site’s address, and none of it is sent anywhere. Unlike a cookie, local storage is not sent with any request: it stays on this device until you clear it. It holds:

  • Your research: your watchlists and which watchlist is active; saved screens and the screener’s current filters; investment cases and the reviews you write of them; saved valuation runs, the valuation assumptions you edit, the required discount you set and the inputs you chose for the valuation sensitivity grid; saved comparisons and the companies you put in a comparison; portfolio holdings and the dividends you record against them; price alerts, and which alert types the feed shows; the companies you recently viewed, and which company reports you opened this month (counted against the plan’s monthly allowance); any prices or statement lines you paste in; the Cash Wheel plan and its legs; withholding-tax settings; and your trading-index observations.
  • Your property work: property inputs and the evidence and register records behind them, with the name or initials you give the register log; the property deal you had before opening a shared link; the prices and rents you record in the comparables register; the locality profiles and demand records you keep on the area screen; the borrower profile you enter for the loan-readiness check (income, commitments and credit conduct); your details for proposals — the name, agency, contact and logo a client proposal prints as yours (a client’s name is never kept); saved property candidates; Sarawak exposure records; the property reports you unlocked in the preview, and the included property reports you used this month.
  • The scanner: your scanner setups with every version of each, which scanner alerts you have read or archived, and your scanner notification and display preferences.
  • Recent: the pages, tools and saved work you recently opened, and when you opened them and each company you viewed — at most twenty, which the search lists when nothing is typed in it; its “Clear recent” forgets them.
  • Records and settings: the data-error cases you record; saved-work snapshots; your answers to the launcher and onboarding questions, and whether you dismissed the introduction; which products’ Start here panels you have hidden; the plan you are previewing; when you last opened your dashboard, so it can count what is new since; a digest of what the pages read of all this — a short fingerprint of each item, never the item itself — so that a page this site sends already drawn is kept out of sight until your own is drawn, where yours differs; and display preferences — your theme and base currency, dashboard layout, table density, how much explanation to show, the language of the property pages, the currency the Compare and screener pages total in, the units for property rates, and whether filed SEC data is switched on.

There are no accounts in this build, so there is nothing to sign in to and no server-side record of you.

What leaves your device

Only requests for this site’s own files: the page itself and the data files it reads — the SEC filings, the instrument list, the Sarawak map and the published NAPIC summary. The security policy this site is served with tells your browser to refuse any request the page itself makes to another address, so this is enforced by your browser, not only promised here.

Each request reaches the company that hosts this site, Vercel, carrying what every web request carries: your IP address, your browser’s name and version, and the address requested. This build does not read or keep those requests; what the host records about them, and for how long, is governed by the host’s own terms.

Some pages keep what is on screen in their address, so that a copied link reproduces it. The property calculator writes the deal’s figures there — the price, the rent and the other inputs you changed, never the borrower profile. An address reaches the host only when a page is loaded with it: a reload, or a link someone opens.

No analytics. This build loads no analytics, advertising or tracking script and counts no page views. If analytics is switched on later, it will come with a consent notice, and this page will say what it records before it records anything.

There is no form on this site that sends anything anywhere. No email address is requested and none can be submitted.

Report a data error records the case in this browser and sends nothing — there is no contact address yet. Its last step hands you the case as text to copy; copying uses your clipboard only when you press the button.

Exporting your data is the only other way anything leaves, and it goes to a file on your own machine when you ask for it.

Links to outside sources — SEC EDGAR, NAPIC, Bursa Malaysia and others — take you to those sites only when you follow one. That visit is between you and that site; the policy this site is served with lets your browser tell it at most this site’s name, never the page you were on.

Cookies

This build sets no cookies, and no response it is served with sets one. Your theme, the plan you are previewing and everything else it remembers are kept in local storage instead, which never travels with a request.

What is never collected

No brokerage credentials, no account numbers, no identity documents. The portfolio feature records what you type and nothing else.

Clearing your data

Clearing this site’s storage in your browser removes everything the product holds about you, immediately and irreversibly. Export first if you want to keep it — Your data lists what is saved and writes it to a file. There is no copy on any server to fall back on.

Personal Data Protection Act 2010 — notice

The notice a data user gives under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia): what personal data is processed and why, where it comes from, who it goes to, and how to ask for access or a correction. It is written for this build, which collects nothing on a server, and for what a launch would add — each addition listed here before it is collected.

Who the data user is

To be supplied: operating entity name, To be supplied: registered address, operating this site at To be supplied: domain.

No operating entity has been registered yet. The data user named here will be responsible for any personal data a launch collects; this build collects none on a server.

What personal data is processed, and where it comes from

Today this build collects none. What you type — including the income, debts and credit record you may enter for the property calculator’s loan-readiness check — comes from you and stays in your browser, where no one else can read it. The only personal data that reaches anyone is what every web request carries to the host — your IP address and your browser’s name and version — as set out above.

A launch would add only what a feature needs, collected from you directly and listed here before it is collected: your email address and message if you write to the contact address; your email address if a waitlist opens; and, if accounts or paid plans are offered, the details they need, with payment handled by a provider named here first.

Purposes

Today there are none, because nothing is collected. At launch, personal data would be used only to answer a message or a correction you send, to send a launch message you asked for, to provide an account or a plan you chose, and to meet a legal obligation. It would not be sold, used for advertising, or used to send you marketing you have not agreed to.

Who it is disclosed to

Today, no one: this build holds nothing to disclose. At launch, only the service providers that run the site for the data user — the host, and an email or payment provider, each named here before it is used — and an authority where the law requires it. The host’s servers may be outside Malaysia; if a launch transfers personal data out of Malaysia, this notice will say where.

Whether you must provide it

Nothing is obligatory. Every page works without you giving any personal data. At launch, if you choose not to give an email address, the only consequence is that no reply or message can reach you.

Access and correction

The Act gives you the right to ask a data user for a copy of the personal data it holds about you, to ask for it to be corrected, to withdraw your consent to its processing, and to require that it is not used for direct marketing.

Today this build holds nothing about you on a server, so there is nothing to request. What it stores is on your device and is yours to see, correct, export and delete — Your data lists it. At launch, send requests to To be supplied: contact email.

Your choices

You can use everything here without giving anything, and export or clear what this browser stores at any time. At launch you would be able to decline any message you did not ask for, and withdraw from one you did.

Security and retention

On your device, anyone who can use this browser profile can read what it stores — clear it on a shared computer. The site’s security policy stops the page itself connecting to any other address. At launch, personal data would be kept only as long as its purpose needs, and the period would be stated here.

Requests and complaints

Write to To be supplied: contact email or to To be supplied: registered address. Until these are supplied there is no one to send a request to — and nothing held to request.

If a request is not resolved, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

Quantum Tradeworks is a research and analysis prototype. It does not provide investment advice, personal recommendations, or a suitability assessment, and it does not execute trades or connect to brokerage accounts. Nothing here is an offer or inducement to buy or sell any security. Financial figures come from two different places and are labelled on every page: statements filed with the US SEC, which are audited and real, and illustrative figures for the Malaysian companies and any other company marked illustrative, which are synthetic and created for interface demonstration. No market-data licence is in place for either exchange. A filed company therefore carries no price at all, and every measure that needs one — market capitalisation, multiples, yield, difference to model estimate — reads as unavailable rather than estimated. Where a price, market capitalisation or yield does appear, it belongs to an illustrative company and is part of that synthetic dataset; it is not a quote and it is not observed from any market. Nothing here may be used for an investment decision. A production deployment would require licensed market data for both markets and, in Malaysia, written legal classification of each surface under the Capital Markets and Services Act before launch.
