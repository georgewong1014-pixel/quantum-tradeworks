The two quotes that are easy to misread

A rate can be quoted two ways that mean very different things, and the loan insurance is usually offered added to the loan rather than paid. Both are decided quickly at the counter and both cost real money.

When a lender says four per cent

On a housing loan, interest is charged only on what you still owe, and what you owe falls every month. On a flat-rate quote the interest is charged on the whole amount for the whole term, however much you have already paid back. The number sounds the same. The cost is not.

Compared below over 5 years on RM25,000, because that is the shape of the borrowing flat rates are used for. Comparing one across a thirty-five year mortgage term would flatter it.

Borrowing RM25,000 over 5 years at your 4.30% would be about 2.26% if it were quoted flat. A renovation lender offering less than that as a flat rate is genuinely cheaper. More, and it is not, whatever the headline says.

The loan insurance, and the cost of borrowing it

Lenders usually ask for cover that settles the loan if you die or become totally disabled. There are two kinds, and the difference is not the price — it is what happens to the cover over time, and what you are left with if you never claim.

QuestionCover that shrinks with the loanCover that stays level
What it pays outFalls as the loan falls. Designed to clear what is still owed, and nothing more.A fixed amount, the same for the whole term, whatever is left on the loan.
Who receives itThe bank, to settle the loan. Your family keeps the house, not a payout.The loan is settled and anything left over goes to your family.
How you payOne premium at the start, usually offered added to the loan.A regular premium, paid separately from the loan.
CostLower, because the cover shrinks over time.Higher, because the cover does not shrink.
Money back if you never claimNone. Early settlement may refund part of the premium, but there is nothing to cash in.Builds an amount you can get back if you stop it.
If you sell, refinance or moveTied to that loan. A new loan means a new policy, at your age then.Yours, and it carries over. Priced at the age you started.
If your health changesA new policy later is assessed on your health at that time.Already in force, so a later change does not reprice it.

The trade names for these are MRTA for the cover that shrinks and MLTA for the cover that stays level, and the takaful equivalents are MRTT and MLTT. No premium is estimated here — it depends on your age, your health, the amount and the term, and it has to come from a quotation. Nothing here is insurance advice.
