What owning this property would do to your cash, from the figures you enter.

What this calculator models

This models the acquisition cost line by line — each fee with its source, estimates marked — then financing, vacancy, maintenance, exit costs and tax, and compares the result with the equity return you enter.

Not a valuation

Not an official property valuation — in Malaysia that must be carried out by a registered valuer. This is an investment estimate built from your inputs and sample transaction data. Figures are scenarios, not predictions.
