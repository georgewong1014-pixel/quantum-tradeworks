## Survey draft (read-only, 9 Nov 2026)

Scope: what each shipped dataset is, where it came from, how old it is, whether it is refreshed, and whether a customer can mistake illustrative or stale figures for current facts. Paths are relative to D:\Download\quantum-tradeworks. Nothing was built, run or modified. Counts came from reading the committed JSON with node (read-only).

## Headline verdict
The data is honest about what it is. The site ships no market prices and no Malaysian statements, and it labels synthetic companies in several places. The weak points are freshness and process. Every dataset is regenerated by hand, none has a schedule, and the one shipped SEC file predates the project's own ingest fixes (sec 1.0.0 shipped, sec 1.2.0 current). The page withholds the known wrong cells at load rather than fixing the file, so the shipped figures are better than the file but not equal to a regeneration.

## 1. Provenance, by dataset

- Licence stated: public domain, "SEC EDGAR company facts sit here" (src/js/19-data-licence.js:42). Company pages print `SEC EDGAR financial data, CIK n` (src/js/35-ui.js:108).

**B. Illustrative set (19 companies: 18 Bursa plus PGR, a US row)**
- Hand-authored in src/js/10-dataset.js, headed "DATASET - synthetic" (10-dataset.js:1-8). Prices are hand-written. Dated `AS_OF = '30 Jul 2026'` (src/js/15-derivation.js:34).
- 119 SEC-filed + 19 illustrative = 138 (15-derivation.js:467-482). PGR has no SEC twin, so one illustrative US row remains.

**C. data/instruments.json - instrument identity (101 per README: 34 Bursa listings, 39 indices, FX, commodities, ETFs, crypto)**
- Hand-edited, "a plain list, not generated" (data/instruments.json `note`). Bursa codes "transcribed from public listings... should be checked against your own broker". Sarawak-flag codes were checked against a live quote lookup on 2026-08-07. Identity only, no prices.

**D. data/napic-h1-2025.json and data/napic-h1-2025/<division>.json (12 divisions: betong, bintulu, kapit, kuching, limbang, miri, mukah, samarahan, sarikei, serian, sibu, sri-aman)**

- Licence stated: `status: REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `publish: DERIVED_ONLY`, attribution "Valuation and Property Services Department (JPPH), Ministry of Finance Malaysia - NAPIC Property Market Report H1 2025", sourceUrl https://napic.jpph.gov.my/en/latest-publication. "Record-level republication, bulk export and raw-file download stay disabled until JPPH confirms commercial redistribution rights." Code states the same (src/js/19-data-licence.js:27-28, 49-62, 84-106).
- Evidence limits are in the ingest header: counts and values by half-year and band, sample-based ranges, no individual transaction date (napic-ingest.mjs:6-30).

**E. data/sarawak-reach.json - DOSM population and OSM places**
- `version 1`, `retrieved 2026-11-01`, `populationAsOf 2026-01-01`. Produced by `ingest/sarawak-reach.mjs`. Sources: DOSM https://storage.dosm.gov.my/population/population_district.csv (ingest/sarawak-reach.mjs:73) and OpenStreetMap via Overpass (ingest/sarawak-reach.mjs:77).

OSM: ODbL 1.0, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors. The file states OSM counts are "at least this many", never totals, and distances are straight-line.

**F. data/sarawak-geo.json - area coordinates**
- One representative point per area, OSM-derived, `retrieved 2026-08-03`, "Redistribution is permitted under ODbL with attribution" (sarawak-geo.json `note`). Produced by `ingest/sarawak-geo.mjs`.

Content-hashed URLs: `DATA_VERSIONS` is injected by build.mjs (src/js/00-core.js:10-20; build.mjs:177-219). A replaced data file always arrives under a new `?v=` hash. This makes updates reach users but does nothing to cause an update.

Automation:
- .github/workflows holds three files: checks.yml, checks-record.yml, journeys.yml. The only cron is journeys.yml:76, `'17 3,15 * * *'` (03:17 and 15:17 UTC). It runs `node journeys.mjs --url production` in Chrome against the live site, and also on `deployment_status` and `workflow_dispatch`. It writes health/journeys.json, health/checks.json and the island pages (journeys.yml:31-50). It does not ingest or refresh any dataset.
- Failure behaviour: the run still writes its job summary, opens or updates ONE issue titled "Production journeys failing", and records its result (journeys.yml:33-39, 162, 233). It exits non-zero on failure (journeys.yml:308-328). GitHub can start a scheduled run 6-7 hours late (journeys.yml:24-28, 1-4 Oct). Beyond the issue and the red run, no alert is sent.
- checks.yml is push and PR driven, offline, with no cron.
- The only data schedule in the repo is ingest/schedule.ps1, a Windows Task Scheduler wrapper for the owner's local daily price capture (task QuantumTradeworks-DailyPrices, 18:30). It needs the machine awake and feeds git-ignored files, so it has no effect on the deployed site.
- Conclusion: all shipped datasets are regenerated by hand. Expected answer confirmed.

Stale-data signal in the UI: `FILINGS_STALE_DAYS = 365` (src/js/35-ui.js:700). Only when us.json `generated` is more than a year old do filing-based tools show "Delayed" with "so an annual report filed since then is not in them" (35-ui.js:520, 732-735). Today the file is 98 days old, so nothing is flagged. The flag would first appear after 3 Aug 2027. By then FY2026 10-Ks of Dec filers (filed Feb-Mar 2027) will have been missing for months. Company headers do print "statements retrieved <date>" (35-ui.js:84-88).

## 3. Filed versus illustrative separation

What exists:
- `dataTag(c)` returns `illustrative`, `personal research` or `SEC-filed` (src/js/35-ui.js:140). `illusChip` puts a visible "illustrative" chip and tooltip on synthetic rows (35-ui.js:127-129), used in screener, heatmap and compare so a synthetic Bursa company does not sit unlabelled beside an audited one (35-ui.js:131-150).
- Company header strip prints Statements FY and period end, `Source` (`SEC EDGAR financial data, CIK n` or `synthetic sample, not a filing`), `As of`, `Price`, `Currency` (35-ui.js:96-120).
- Price label: illustrative prices read "sample price, 30 Jul 2026", never a time-stamped close (35-ui.js:73-83). A filed company with no price reads "none supplied". 115 filed companies hold no price (35-ui.js:69-72; coverage `filedUnpriced`, 15-derivation.js:525-526).
- Peers never mix: a filed company is compared only with filed peers in its market, an illustrative one only with illustrative ones, peers named (src/js/20-valuation.js:595-610).
- No seeded price walk or invented documents for a filed company (src/js/25-universe.js:466-475, 581-587).
- Illustrative company pages and reports are `noindex` (35-ui.js:1548-1556; owner decision 2026-10-03).
- Coverage manifest: `coverage()` splits filed / personal / illustrative and US / MY, computed in one place, with a "still counting" state while filings load (15-derivation.js:467-583). `coverageSentence` states the PGR oddity ("Not every illustrative company is Malaysian", 15-derivation.js:577-580).
- Tool badge: Equities is Beta, qualified "Filed + illustrative data" (35-ui.js:398-408). Instruments carry `dataStatus` FILED / ILLUSTRATIVE / UNAVAILABLE (src/js/26-instruments.js:69, 102).
- No claim of live market data: "No licensed market prices" (35-ui.js:406); "No price source is connected at any tier" (src/js/65-learn.js:390); the deployed site "carries no prices and no Malaysian statements" (25-universe.js:13-14).

Residual risks:
- The label is a chip plus tooltip. Where a Bursa row shows with only a ticker (instrument search, watchlists, recents, reports, scanner universe) it depends on `dataStatus` or `dataTag` being applied. I did not check each surface in a browser (see F4).
- PGR is a real NYSE ticker with synthetic figures (15-derivation.js:475-482). A reader who knows Progressive would take it as real.

## 4. Stale and missing data handling

- Missing line: null stays null and shows "n/a" with tooltip "Not available or not meaningful for this company type" (src/js/00-core.js:70). A figure that exists but is deliberately not computed shows "withheld" (00-core.js:71-74).
- Splits: per-share growth and dilution metrics are withheld when shares move more than 1.5x or below 0.67x in the window (src/js/13-metrics.js:177-212, 286-335). Share counts are "not adjusted for splits" (13-metrics.js:180; 15-derivation.js:1229; 65-learn.js:400).
- Zero or negative denominators: CAGR returns null on a non-positive base, "n/m rather than a fake number" (src/js/35-ui.js, `cagr`, directly after `provenance`). ROE is withheld when equity is under 5% of revenue or changes sign (13-metrics.js:144; 15-derivation.js:771-798). EBIT above revenue withholds margins and returns (13-metrics.js:105-129; 15-derivation.js:755). Dividend-to-earnings scale mismatches are withheld (15-derivation.js:975-985). A model with no price returns an explicit reason, and "NO PRICE IS NOT A ZERO PRICE" (20-valuation.js:240-244, 491-496). Unavailable lines are named: total assets, current assets and liabilities, D&A "are not held" (13-metrics.js:429-447).
- Stale-file repair: `withholdMisassembled` nulls shares resting on `CommonStockSharesIssued` (counts treasury stock), debt that is a current portion alone, and `LongTermDebt` plus the current portion double-counted, and blanks three misfiled sector labels as "Unclassified" (25-universe.js:755-800). 331 share cells in the shipped file rest on `CommonStockSharesIssued`. A file with `basis` skips this (25-universe.js:770).
- Currency and units: billions of the reporting currency, shares in billions (10-dataset.js:5-8). US rows USD, Bursa illustrative rows MYR. Cross-market views use one number, `FX.USDMYR`, default 4.42 with `source: 'sample'` and no date (05-plans.js:274, 284-298). Discover prints "Indicative sample rate - no observation date - nobody observed it" (40-views-discover.js:91-95) on one card. A supplied rate is plausibility-banded (25-universe.js:951-955).

## 5. Duplicates, corporate actions, caching, corrections

- Duplicates: 119 unique ids and CIKs. The seventeen illustrative twins of filed US companies are retired on load (`retireIllustrativeTwin`, 15-derivation.js:467-482; 35-ui.js:2876-2878). PGR is the one un-retired illustrative US row. The XOM CIK trap is handled by `CIK_OVERRIDES` (ingest/README.md failure mode 1).
- Corporate actions: none held. No price-adjustment file ships (ingest/history-check.mjs expects data/price-adjustments.json; absent from data/). Imported bars are `adjusted: "unknown"`. Share counts are unadjusted.
- Caching: no service worker (no `serviceWorker` or `sw.js` in src, index.html or build.mjs; no sw*.js outside node_modules). vercel.json sets `public, max-age=31536000, immutable` on content-addressed data (vercel.json:1497, 1524, 1541, 1558, 1575). Files outside `VERSIONED` keep no-store (00-core.js:19-20). Because the hash follows content, a data update does reach users.
- Corrections log (src/js/65-learn.js:695-740): the code comment says every row is a shipped correction and the earlier invented rows were removed. Rows seen (25 Oct back to 28 Sep 2026) are Property fee and model changes and a metric dictionary row. I found no row about the US data: the withheld share, debt and sector cells, or the pending regeneration. Reader-submitted corrections save to local storage only (65-learn.js:825, 1261, 1342).

## 6. Known gap: ingest year-end fix versus the shipped data

Confirmed, and wider than a date mismatch.
- ingest/sec.mjs:56-61: "sec 1.0.0 the original ingest (data/us.json, August 2026 - unstamped)"; "sec 1.1.0 an instant is the balance at the fiscal year-end (26 Sep 2026)"; "sec 1.2.0 a unit the line does not expect is refused; a line whose period disagrees with the income statement's year-end is nulled; a share count of nought is no count; restatements identified". `INGEST_VERSION = 'sec 1.2.0'`.
- data/us.json carries no `ingestVersion` or `basis` on header or records (checked on all 119), so it was built by 1.0.0, before both fixes.
- Regeneration is blocked by the missing SEC contact address: data-check.mjs:27-30 ("THE FILE CANNOT BE REGENERATED HERE") and 25-universe.js:757-759. Matches the memory note (needs an `SEC_UA` email).
- Known defects the file still carries (data-check.mjs:45-47): EMR dividend per share filed under unit "pure" and used as USD per share, so payout and yield are computed from it; CI FY2016-17 share counts of nought (engine reads these as absent).
- The year-end defect (balance instants possibly taken at a quarter-end) cannot be located cell by cell, because the file holds no fiscal-year-end basis. The page says "period end not yet held" (35-ui.js:102-104).
- `data-check.mjs` fails the release check if a new finding appears or a KNOWN finding stops occurring, so the list is controlled.

## 8. Findings, ranked

**F1 High - The only shipped fundamentals file predates the project's own ingest fixes.**
Evidence: no `ingestVersion` or `basis` in data/us.json; sec.mjs:56-61 lists the 26 Sep fix and 1.2.0; data-check.mjs:27-47 and 25-universe.js:755-800 admit regeneration is blocked and cells are withheld at load. A customer reading "audited SEC filings" sees figures built by superseded rules.
Minimum action: supply `SEC_UA`, regenerate with ingest/sec.mjs, run data-check and the release check, commit. If that cannot happen before launch, say on /data-sources and in each source drawer that figures were extracted in August 2026 under rules since corrected and some lines are withheld.

**F2 High - The stale threshold is a year and nothing refreshes data.**
Evidence: `FILINGS_STALE_DAYS = 365` (35-ui.js:700); the only cron updates health records (journeys.yml:76). Filers with June or September year-ends (Microsoft, Nvidia, Walmart, Oracle, Nike) already have newer 10-Ks than the file holds.
Minimum action: name an owner and a regeneration date in the launch runbook; lower the Delayed threshold to about 120 days or print the retrieval date wherever a filed figure is shown (company header already does).

**F3 High - NAPIC benchmarks are about 16 months old and the licence is unresolved.**
Evidence: napic-h1-2025.json `period` and `licence`; 19-data-licence.js:49-62. Property results show price and rent ranges a buyer may read as current.
Minimum action: confirm every Property result prints "NAPIC H1 2025 (to 30 Jun 2025)" beside its benchmark, and that JPPH's answer or counsel covers publishing aggregates. State that later half-years are not loaded.

**F4 Medium - Illustrative figures may be mistaken for filed ones where only a ticker shows.**
Evidence: chip is confirmed in screener, heatmap, compare and the header strip (35-ui.js:127-150); other surfaces not browser-checked. PGR is a real ticker with synthetic figures (15-derivation.js:475-482).
Minimum action: sweep watchlists, recents, reports and export, saved work, decision record and scanner universe for an `illusChip` or `dataStatus` label. Remove PGR or rename it so it cannot read as the real company.

**F5 Medium - Undated sample FX rate sits under cross-market figures.**
Evidence: 05-plans.js:274; the label appears on one Discover card only (40-views-discover.js:91-95); conversions at 05-plans.js:284-298.
Minimum action: show "indicative 4.42, undated" beside every converted figure, or disable conversion while `FX.source === 'sample'`.

**F6 Medium - The corrections log has no row for the US data corrections.**
Evidence: 65-learn.js:704-740 lists Property and metric rows only; the withholding and sector relabelling (25-universe.js:755-800) change what customers see as filed.
Minimum action: add dated rows for the withheld cells and the pending regeneration.

**F7 Medium - EMR dividend unit defect is not repaired on the page.**
Evidence: data-check.mjs:46. Payout and yield treat a "pure"-unit value as USD.
Minimum action: withhold EMR dividend in the loader, or regenerate (F1).

**F8 Low - DOSM licence rests on the portal statement; the terms page was unreadable.**
Evidence: sarawak-reach.json `licenceBasis`; ingest/sarawak-reach.mjs:22-29. Action: keep attribution visible and record who will check the terms page.

**F9 Low - No corporate-action basis for any price; share counts unadjusted.**
Evidence: `adjusted: "unknown"`; 65-learn.js:400. Mitigated because no price history deploys and split-sensitive metrics are withheld (13-metrics.js:177-335). Action: none for launch.

**F10 Low - Map and reach data are developer-run with no schedule.**
Evidence: map-assets.mjs, sarawak-reach.mjs. Dates are held in the manifest and the file, and OSM counts are labelled "at least". Action: keep those dates visible on the pages.
