## Gate result

- **No misleading price**: none is rendered; the proposed figures live unrendered in the public bundle (owner decision D-10 to keep or strip).
- **No unavailable feature sold as available**: monitoring, delivery channels, hosted scans and paid reports are all stated unavailable/proposed.
- **Mismatches remaining are low-severity copy items** (name collision, three scanner phrasings, recovery sentence, Terms blanket sentence) and are listed for owner or counsel decision, not corrected in this audit (the 1 Nov freeze; pinned copy).
- The optional /pricing sentence proposed on 9 Nov ("Proposed features and limits are shown for comparison only") remains an owner decision; the page already says free-in-beta, not-on-sale, no-payment in three places.

Commercial alignment gate: **passes for the free beta**; **No-Go for both paid offers** (no approved pricing, no payment processing, no delivery ownership).
