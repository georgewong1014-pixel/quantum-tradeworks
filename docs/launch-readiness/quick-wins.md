# Quick wins inside the private tools — Quantum Tradeworks

Internal-tools audit, 10 November 2026. "Private tools" here are the submission function, the test harnesses, CI and the operator paths; the public product is under the 1 November freeze. Each item is a defect or gap found during this audit or the certification audit of 9 November, with its evidence. Ten are listed; seven are implemented, three deferred with the reason.
