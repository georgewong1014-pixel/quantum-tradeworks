Bring your own prices

What happens to what you paste

It stays in this browser.

  • Held in this browser’s local storage, on this device. It is not uploaded, not sent to a server, and not visible to anyone else — there are no accounts and no server to send it to.
  • Every figure derived from it is labelled as yours rather than treated as a source of record. A price you supplied and a licensed close are not the same evidence.
  • Clearing your browser data removes it. There is no copy anywhere else, so export it if it took work to assemble.
  • Whatever you paste remains under whatever terms you obtained it. This tool does not acquire it, republish it, or give you a right to share it — a broker export is still your broker’s data.
