What works today

There is no contact address yet — no email, no form and no telephone number — so nothing you write on this site reaches anyone.

One route does work. Report a data error, here and in the footer of every page, records a wrong figure as a case in this browser, with an identifier, the page you were on, the model version and the data date. It does not send the case: the last step hands it to you as text to copy, and every case you record is listed under Corrections, ready to send once there is somewhere to send it.

The corrections log

The corrections log records corrections made to this build — what was wrong and what changed, dated by the change. A case you record is not added to it, because nobody has received it.
