Contact

How to reach the site’s owner, who handles support and corrections — and what does not exist yet.

What exists today

Write to georgewong1014@gmail.com, the site owner’s support address. It opens your own email program; this site does not send the message or confirm that it arrived.

Report a data error, here and in the footer of every page, records a wrong figure as a case in this browser, with an identifier, the page you were on, the model version and the data date. Its last step gives you the report to copy and send to that address from your own email program; the case stays recorded here, not confirmed received, and is listed under Corrections.

Report a data error

What to include

The company or property, the figure, what you believe it should be, and where that comes from. A source makes a correction verifiable rather than a disagreement. Do not include confidential or sensitive information.

The corrections log

The corrections log records corrections made to this product — what was wrong and what changed, dated by the change. A case you record or send is not added to it; a correction it leads to is.
