Record a snapshot above and this page shows four things for each area and property type, kept apart:

  • Supply: how many listings you counted, and how that changed since the snapshot before.
  • Supply asking price: the median of the asking prices you saw. Asking is what sellers hope for.
  • Demand: how many sales you recorded in the period. NAPIC’s count for the whole division and half-year is shown beside it as context: a different measure on a wider geography, never added to yours.
  • Demand bidding price: the median of the transacted prices you recorded. A transacted price is where a buyer and a seller met, not a bid, and real bids are not public.

A median shows once 3 or more are recorded; below that the page shows the count and says there are too few. An area with nothing recorded reads “No record”, never nought. Where both sides are recorded for the same area, type and period, the page also shows the gap between asking and transacted prices and the months of supply.

Sales and rents are recorded on the comparables register, and count here once they carry an area. Nothing is fetched from Mudah, PropertyGuru or any other listing site.
