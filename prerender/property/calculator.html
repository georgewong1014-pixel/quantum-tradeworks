What this calculator models

This models the acquisition cost line by line — each fee with its source, estimates marked — then financing, vacancy, maintenance, exit costs and tax, and compares the result with an assumed equity return.

Not a valuation Results are calculated from your inputs and labelled assumptions. They are not a property valuation or investment recommendation.
Why this is not a valuation

Not an official property valuation — in Malaysia that must be carried out by a registered valuer. This is an investment estimate built from your inputs and sample transaction data. Figures are scenarios, not predictions.
